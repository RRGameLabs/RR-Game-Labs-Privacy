Privacy Policy for RR Game Labs Games

Effective Date: October 24, 2025

RR Game Labs (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you play any of our games available on the Apple App Store.

Games Covered Under This Policy

  • Zone Brawl.io – a multiplayer arena and territory battle game.

Information We Collect
